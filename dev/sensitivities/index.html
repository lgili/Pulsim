    Parameter sensitivities¶

    Exact derivatives of a simulated waveform, and of KPIs computed from it, with respect to circuit parameters. They are available on the dsed exact stepper and on the time-stepping engines pwl and trbdf2 (see "On the time-stepping engines" below). Every other route refuses with a typed pulsim.UnsupportedError. No gradient is ever a finite difference presented as exact.

    import pulsim as p

b = p.CircuitBuilder()
b.add_voltage_source("Vin", "vin", "gnd", 24.0)
b.add_switch("S", "vin", "sw", 1e3, 1e-9)
b.add_diode("D1", "gnd", "sw", 1e3, 1e-9)
b.add_inductor("L", "sw", "vout", 100e-6)
b.add_capacitor("C", "vout", "gnd", 47e-6)
b.add_resistor("RL", "vout", "gnd", 50.0)
pwm = p.make_pwm_switch_fn(100e3, 0.3, b.switch_index_of("S"),
                           b.graph.num_switches)

res = p.simulate(b, 400e-6, engine="dsed", switch_fn=pwm,
                 sensitivities=["L.L", "RL.R", "S.duty"])
res.sens("vout", "L.L")               # d v_out / d L at every sample
v, g = res.kpi("vout", "avg", t_from=300e-6)   # value and {name: grad}

orbit = p.periodic_steady_state(b, period=1e-5, switch_fn=pwm,
                                sensitivities=["L.L", "S.duty"])
orbit.dx0["S.duty"]                   # d x* / d D at the section
orbit.kpi("vout", "ripple")           # orbit KPI and its gradient

    What is differentiated¶

    The map is the dsed exact stepper with its events located (rtol = 1e-11) and without the event projection. This is the map that periodic_steady_state uses. The sensitivity run can therefore differ from a plain dsed run where the projection would act.

    Between events, every step is the exact LTI propagator exp(A h). Its derivative is formed in one of two ways:

    • in the eigenbasis (Daleckii-Krein with phi1 divided differences);
    • by the Van Loan block exponential on a near-defective mode.

    The pass re-derives every step and compares it with the engine's sample. A step it cannot reproduce refuses the run.

    The derivatives dA/dp, db/dp and the node-voltage recovery come from assemblies of the circuit, and they are exact for R, L, C and DC source values. The duty is differentiable on a fixed-period trailing-edge PWM: dtau/dD = T on each falling edge.

    The steady-state gradient is dx*/dp = (I - M)^-1 dP/dp. It costs one more period run, which n_period_runs counts.

    Names¶

    A parameter name has the form "<component>.<field>", where the field is one of:

    • R, L, C or V;
    • the components() key, such as R_ohms;
    • "<switch>.duty".

    The following are refused, and the refusal names the device and the field:

    • switch conductances;
    • diode thresholds;
    • Newton-device parameters;
    • integer, topology and table parameters.

    KPIs¶

    res.kpi(signal, kind, t_from=, t_to=) takes the following kinds:

    kind value
    final value at t_to
    avg time-weighted average
    rms RMS
    energy the integral
    peak maximum
    valley minimum
    ripple peak - valley

    Integrals are closed forms of the exact trajectory. A signal that jumps at an event, such as a switch node, adds the term (y- - y+) dtau/dp. Value and gradient are of the same quantity, so a central difference of the value agrees to rounding.

    non_unique marks a peak or valley that is attained at two separate instants. There the function is not differentiable, and the gradient given is that of the first instant.

    RMS, peak and ripple over a near-defective mode are refused in this release.

    The event rule¶

    A gradient exists only while the event pattern is locally constant.

    • Event time. At a diode event, dtau/dp = -(grad g . S- + g_p) / (grad g . f-), and the sensitivity jumps by (f- - f+) dtau/dp.
    • Grazing is refused. An event that is tangent to its switching surface (|grad g . f-| <= 1e-9 |grad g| |f-|) is refused with feature grazing_event, naming the diode: one side of the parameter removes the event.
    • Near misses are flagged. An armed predicate can come within 1e-6 of its range of zero without crossing, for example at the CCM/DCM boundary. The gradient is then computed, because it is exact for the pattern taken. It is also flagged with a UserWarning, and the details go in res.sensitivity.flags.

    Discrete against continuous gradient¶

    The gradient is that of the exact, continuous-time trajectory: the limit of the simulation as the step and event tolerances go to zero. It is not the derivative of a fixed-step engine's discrete output.

    At one step per T/100, the pwl engine's finite differences of v_out are wrong by factors up to 13. The reason is that its edges snap to the grid. Use the exact path to optimise. Then verify the optimum on any engine.

    Optimisation¶

    p.value_and_grad(objective, names) wraps an objective that returns (value, {name: grad}), or a KpiValue, into the f(x) -> (value, grad) form that scipy.optimize.minimize(jac=True) expects. A missing gradient raises an error.

    pulsim.design.Evaluator(..., gradients={"L": "L.L"}) adds exact gradients of v_out, ripple_v and ripple_i.<L> to record.grads on the pss route. The following are refused with feature gradients:

    • the transient route;
    • the thermal loop;
    • a design variable that is not the circuit value itself, or that sets more than one field.

    A KPI whose extremum is not unique (the flat inductor-current valley of a DCM converter) gets no gradient. Its name is listed in record.counters["grads_refused"].

    On the time-stepping engines¶

    simulate(engine="pwl" | "trbdf2", sensitivities=[...]) carries the stage recurrence of the step the run took (TR-BDF2, SDIRK, ESDIRK43 and the fixed engine's trapezoid / restart stages). Across an event the charges and fluxes are continuous and their sensitivity jumps:

    dq/dp+ = dq/dp- + (dq/dt- - dq/dt+) * dtau/dp

    with dq/dt- the last pre-event stage's own field and dq/dt+ the field at the consistent post-event point (one bordered solve on the first post-event stage's factor; no extra factorization). dtau/dp is

    • -(grad g . S-) / (grad g . dx/dt-) at a PWL diode crossing,
    • T at the OFF edge of <switch>.duty (a fixed-period trailing-edge PWM from make_pwm_switch_fn, driving that switch alone), 0 at its ON edge,
    • 0 at a gate edge of a fixed schedule (no jump; carried even without event splitting).

    Carried: diode crossings on pwl with enable_substep_state_correction=True (the crossing is located and the step split there) and on trbdf2 (the step lands on the located crossing); duty and fixed gate edges on both.

    Refused by name (UnsupportedError, feature sensitivities unless noted):

    • a mode change that was not located in time: pwl without event splitting (the error names the diode: the solution is a staircase in the parameter), a duty edge taken at a grid point, a commutation inside an SDIRK stage, an event-iteration breach, a retried step;
    • two events on one instant that move differently with the parameters (a tie), and a grazing crossing (feature grazing_event);
    • an event whose instant moves in a circuit with a Newton device (the consistent post-event field needs a nonlinear re-initialisation);
    • an event after which the charges cannot stay continuous (an impulsive commutation: the state jumps);
    • on trbdf2: an interpolated or ESDIRK diode landing, a crossing within the conditioning floor of a step start (flipped by probes, not landed), a slot-only derivative refresh near t_end or a tick;
    • stateful devices (saturable inductor, nonlinear capacitor, Lauritzen diode, machines, IGBT tail, external devices), resume_from, start_from_dc_op, in-kernel losses, callbacks and closed loops.

    Cost¶

    One engine run, plus one propagation per parameter per step and four assemblies per element parameter. Eight parameters cost one run, against sixteen by central differences. res.sensitivity.counters reports the counts.
