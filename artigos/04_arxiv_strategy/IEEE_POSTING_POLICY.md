## What we rely on

The Author Posting Policy distinguishes three manuscript versions:

1. **Preprint** — the version submitted to IEEE for review.
2. **Accepted version** — the post-peer-review, pre-typeset version.
   Authors' own LaTeX/Word formatting.
3. **Final published version** — the IEEE-typeset PDF in IEEE Xplore.

IEEE permits authors to post **versions 1 and 2** on personal
websites, employer/institutional repositories, and not-for-profit
preprint servers (arXiv qualifies). IEEE prohibits posting
**version 3** outside Xplore.

## Verbatim policy text (as captured 2026-05-24)

The following is reproduced from IEEE's public policy pages for
reference. Authoritative source is always the live IEEE page —
re-fetch before each submission.

> ### Posting your article — Submitted versions
> Authors may post their submitted version (preprint) of an
> article on the author's personal web site or on their employer's
> web site. Authors may also post the submitted version on
> not-for-profit preprint servers such as TechRxiv or arXiv.

> ### Posting your article — Accepted versions
> Authors may post the accepted version of an article on the
> author's personal web site, the author's employer's web site,
> and on not-for-profit servers such as arXiv. Authors shall
> not post the final, published versions of their papers.

> ### IEEE copyright notice
> When posting or re-using the article, authors must provide a
> copyright notice in the form:
>
> "© 20XX IEEE. Personal use of this material is permitted.
> Permission from IEEE must be obtained for all other uses, in
> any current or future media, including reprinting/republishing
> this material for advertising or promotional purposes, creating
> new collective works, for resale or redistribution to servers
> or lists, or reuse of any copyrighted component of this work in
> other works."
>
> A link to the article's DOI must also be provided.

## Practical implication for the Pulsim pipeline

- ✅ Post the **accepted manuscript** (our LaTeX-compiled
     post-review version) to arXiv.
- ✅ Include the verbatim copyright notice above on the arXiv PDF
     and the arXiv abstract page Comments field.
- ✅ Include the IEEE DOI as `DOI: 10.1109/TPEL.XXXX.XXXXXXX` in
     the same Comments field.
- ❌ Do not upload the IEEE-typeset PDF (received post-acceptance
     for proofreading).
- ❌ Do not re-license under CC-BY or any other open-content
     license; the arXiv non-exclusive distribution licence is
     compatible, anything else is not.
