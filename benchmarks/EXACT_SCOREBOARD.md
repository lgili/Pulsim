Generated by `benchmarks/tools/exact_scoreboard.py render` from `run` records (do not edit). Every circuit the exact truth solves (conductance form) x the five harness configurations, graded with the `exact` measure against the truth (`benchmarks/tools/exact_truth.py`), beside the strict `interp` verdict (unchanged).

`exact`: per graded column, sqrt(sum w_i e_i^2 / sum w_i) / span over the engine's own samples (w_i trapezoid time weights, first sample excluded), e_i the distance to the nearest one-sided truth value at t_i (every truth segment within 0.001 dt, continued to t_i; a sample matched across an event is charged jump^2 x its time gap, the true L2 of that displacement, beyond 4 ulp), span the truth's peak-to-peak (floored at 1e-3 of its peak). Input columns (only a source in them) are not graded. Cell = worst column; a non-finite sample scores inf. No interpolation of the engine trace: what it does between its samples is not graded, so `measure` below means the engine's samples are right (the strict gate's interpolation floor, or its reference, is what disagrees).

No floor: the truth itself (mpmath `Solver.sample`) graded on each engine's own grid scores at most 9.1e-14 over 486 grids (median 7.1e-17).
