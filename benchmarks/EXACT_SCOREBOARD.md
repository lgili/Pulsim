## The measure

`exact` (`benchmarks/tools/metrics.py`, `exact_parts`), per graded column, three parts, each a fraction of `span` (the truth's peak-to-peak, floored at 1e-3 of its peak):

- **smooth**: sqrt(sum w_i e_i^2 / sum w_i) over the engine's STORED rows, e_i the distance to the truth at t_i (two one-sided candidates only when t_i is a truth event instant; either is right), w_i trapezoid time weights; left out: the initial instant, the right limit at t = 0+ (stored rows only), and the rows inside a matched event's displacement [min(t_engine, t_truth), max(...)] (the engine is on the other branch there: `instants` prices it; the row at the engine's instant is its left limit: `limits` grades it). Every other row is graded, the first one solved after an event included. No snap window, no timing charge.
- **instants**: engine device events (changes of the switch record of `pulsim.with_event_limits(res)`: stored rows, the split's inner events, right limits; the instant is the row before the change) matched to the truth's by device and direction, in order, the nearest within 2 netlist steps; cost sqrt(sum_e jump_e^2 |delta_e| / T), the true L2 of each displacement (jump from the truth's two limits; |delta| within 4 ulp counts 0); a missing truth event is charged as displaced to that device's next truth event, at most the window (beyond it, the rows carry the error). `max δ/h`: the largest |delta| in units of the netlist dt, the same unit for every engine (the fixed engine's step; the output grid of the variable-step and exact engines, which place events by root finding). Missing and spurious events are counted and named in the json; a spurious one is not charged a duration (no row is left out around it: its effect is in `smooth`, its values in `limits`); an event displaced by more than the window is a missing + spurious pair. `n/a`: the run exposes no switch record (every row is then graded).
- **limits**: the largest distance of a one-sided value the engine records after t = 0 (its left value at each of its event instants, every right-limit row, matched or not) from the truth's limit on that side at the truth's instant (at the engine's own instant when there is no match). An engine without right limits (trbdf2) is graded on its left values only.

Cell = the largest part of the worst column (`by` names it); a non-finite value anywhere scores inf. Classes (< 1e-3 / 1e-6 / 1e-9) are read from it. Events at the run's first or last instant are not graded. Source breaks without a device flip are not events here: the engine is given their instant, and the rows around them are graded in `smooth`; a non-finite truth value scores inf. Input columns (only a source in them) are not graded.

**What changed and why (this release).** The previous `exact` (kept as `exact_old`, column `old`) was one time-weighted RMS over every row of the two-sided trace with a snap window of 1e-3 dt. Two flaws: (1) it dropped only rows at t == t[0], so the right limit at t = 0+ (5e-324) was graded against the truth's initial value: on `three_phase_diode_rectifier` pwl+restart that one row (V(out) 17.22 V, a quadrature line start, against 0 V) was 99 % of the score, 0.0127 against 0.000998 without it; the strict gate already drops it. (2) An event the engine places delta away from the truth's, beyond the snap window, was charged jump^2 x the row's trapezoid weight instead of jump^2 x |delta|: `vcswitch_resistor` pwl+split switches 0.005 h early and read 0.0316 (half a step, 100x the true cost in the square; 0 before the right-limit rows existed), against 0.0032 now; adding or removing a row at the event changed the charge by a factor unrelated to accuracy. Both flaws mispriced all engines whenever an event is displaced; the snap window and its charge are gone.

No floor: the truth itself (mpmath `Solver.sample`) graded by the smooth part on each engine's own rows scores at most 9.2e-14 over 810 grids (median 7.3e-17).
