## Shipped examples against an independent truth

Each example is run in the route survey's setting (`survey`: no switch_fn, every switch closed) and, where the file documents a switch_fn, in that one (`doc`: the header's frequency and duty; the PSFB at phi = pi/2 with 100 ns dead time). Error = the worst column's time-weighted RMS over [dt, t_end] of the piecewise-linear traces, relative to that column's peak (a column at ~0 is judged against 1e-6 of its unit's largest peak); exact's trace carries both sides of every jump (`with_right_limits`). A closed form is evaluated on each engine's own samples, so an engine is not charged for drawing straight lines between them. The truth's uncertainty is its distance to the second, coarser truth run. auto = the example's own dt on pwl (the one rule: a dt in the file is a fixed step). The old column (the largest pointwise difference from sample 1 divided by the column's peak) compared a left limit with a right limit at every edge and divided noise by ~0 peaks; it is gone.
